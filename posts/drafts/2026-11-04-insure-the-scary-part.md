In the US you can insure a $15,000 hole-in-one prize for about $499. Organisers then sell that hole to a sponsor for $1,000 to $1,500.

The insurer carries the risk. The sponsor buys the moment. The organiser keeps the difference. One company has paid out over $64 million in prizes since 1991 and is still in business, so the maths works.

That's the model under Get Lucky. Every prize on our par 3s is underwritten by Santam before anyone tees it up. We don't sell odds, we sell a moment a brand wants its name on.

Simple business. Insure the scary part. Sell the fun part.
