A floating tee on a lake. $1 a ball, $10,000 if you hole it. Someone worked out it does about $600k a year.

Same model in New Zealand. Same model in Hong Kong, with LIV and HSBC and Bryson hitting into the harbour.

Sell a cheap shot at a big prize. Insure the prize. Put it where people already are.

We do it on par 3s in South Africa. Next it goes indoors onto simulators, because a screen doesn't care about the weather.

Simple enough to explain to a 10-year-old. Big enough to run on every par 3 on earth.

That's the kind of business I like.
