We put a R1 million prize on a par 3 and asked golfers to pay R50 for one swing.

That's the whole business. One hole, one QR code on the tee, one swing.

Here is what launching it taught me, because very little of this was in the plan:

1. Every step between a golfer and the tee costs you entries. No app. No membership. No handicap. Scan, pay, swing. If it takes longer than ordering a beer, you've lost them.

2. The prize is not the product. The insurance is. Santam and Indwe underwrite every rand before anyone swings, so a club carries zero risk and a golfer knows the money is real. Without that it's a raffle. With it, it's a platform.

3. Cameras beat arguments. Solar-powered 4G cameras capture every attempt. Verified hole-in-ones are paid within 15 working days. Nobody has to take anyone's word for it.

4. Adding a club used to be a project. Now it's a config file. 32 branded club storefronts, same code underneath. When adding a product costs nothing, you stop rationing and let the market decide.

We're live at 30+ courses across South Africa. R50 to R1,000 a swing. R25,000 to R1,000,000 if you sink it.

Launch, learn, repeat. The plan on paper was wrong in four places. The only way to find out was to open the tee box.
