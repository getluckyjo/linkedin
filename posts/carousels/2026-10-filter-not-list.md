kicker: The right order
bone
# Score the company first. Then find the person inside it.
Sales Navigator finds decision makers fine. It doesn't tell you if the company is worth a meeting.
---
kicker: Step one
# Make the pitch state a number.
There's a minimum spend below which the deal can't work, whatever anyone wants. Until it's a rand figure, "big customer" is a feeling and every list is a guess.
---
kicker: Step two
bone
# Find the one admin question that tells you the size.
People answer admin questions without feeling interrogated. Ask it before you book anything and most of the wasted meetings disappear.
---
kicker: Step three
# Stop spending the free report too early.
Gate it on the two answers, not on a meeting. Anyone who answers has qualified themselves.
---
kicker: Test it
bone
# Run it on three companies you already know.
One you'd love, one you'd pass on, one you're unsure about. If it ranks them the way you would, trust it on four hundred you've never heard of.
