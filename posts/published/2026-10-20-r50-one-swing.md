R50 for one swing at R25,000. R1,000 for a swing at R1 million.

That's Get Lucky. A QR code on a par 3, a camera on a pole, Santam underwriting the prize.

30 courses in, three things I had wrong on paper:

Friction. If entering takes longer than ordering a beer, they're gone. No app, no membership, no handicap.

The prize. Golfers don't buy odds. They buy the story for the 19th. R50 sells. R1,000 sells too.

The clubs. I thought they'd want to be paid to host it. They want zero risk and zero admin. Give them that and they say yes.

Launch, learn, repeat. The only way to find out was to open the tee box.
