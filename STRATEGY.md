# The 100,000 plan

Johannes Le Roux, Cape Town. Six brands built, two sold internationally, one golf-tech platform scaling now, a coaching practice on the side. The LinkedIn job: become the South African voice on *launch, learn, repeat* entrepreneurship. Simple businesses, big thinking, real money.

## The position (say it the same way every time)

> I build simple businesses that make money, then I show you how. Launch, learn, repeat.

Headline today: "Building Get Lucky Golf | Coaching a handful of exceptional founders | ex-Duchess, ex-DOPE". Keep it. Add "Launch, learn, repeat." if the length allows.

## Three pillars, one post each per week

| Day | Pillar | What it is | Where the material comes from |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | **Build in public** | What happened at Get Lucky, Entrepreneur Coach, Sold Direct this week. Numbers, mistakes, wins. | Weeks tab of the sheet (private) |
| Wed | **Ideas and models** | A business model from somewhere in the world, taken apart, with the Johannes angle. | `research/ideas.md` |
| Fri | **Offers and lessons** | A lesson from The Duchess, DOPE, Brannas or the coaching work, with a soft offer: clinic, workshop, sprint. | entrepreneurcoach.co.za journal and packages |

Every post ends with one question or one offer. Never both.

## Voice rules

- First line is the hook. A specific number or a sentence that makes someone stop. "I can't code. Since March I've shipped 17 things."
- Paragraphs of one to three sentences. White space is free.
- Confident, warm, direct. A South African founder talking to another founder at the 19th.
- Real numbers, his own brands, real places. Never invent a stat. "Roughly" plus a source beats made-up precision.
- No "thrilled", "excited to announce", "humbled", "game-changer". No em dashes. Hashtags: zero to three, at the very end, only if useful.
- Afrikaans is welcome when the story is Afrikaans. Don't translate the business terms.
- 900 to 1,300 characters is the sweet spot. Hard max 3,000.
- One idea per post. If there are two, that's two posts.

## Cadence

- 3 posts a week, Mon/Wed/Fri at 07:12 SAST, automated from `posts/queue/`.
- Johannes's manual job, 20 minutes a day: reply to every comment on his post within 90 minutes of 07:12, and leave 5 real comments (two sentences, an opinion) on posts by people on the current `contacts/` list. No API can do this and it is half the growth.
- Sunday: the engine drafts next week. Johannes edits or deletes anything by Monday 07:00. Silence means go.

## Growth math (honest version)

2026 creator data: accounts posting 3 to 4 times a week with daily commenting move from low thousands to about 25k in roughly 8 months. "100k in 6 months" claims assume 5 posts a week, carousels, video and a running start. Growth compounds in months 4 to 6; most people quit in month 3.

Working plan from an unknown start (Johannes to confirm the current count in the Metrics tab):

| Milestone | Target | What has to be true |
|---|---|---|
| 10k | Month 6 | 3 text posts a week, daily comments, a profile that converts (banner, pinned post, featured links) |
| 30k | Month 12 | Add 1 carousel or short video a week, one collaboration post a month with a bigger SA voice |
| 60k | Month 18 | Weekly LinkedIn newsletter ("Launch, Learn, Repeat"), a repeatable format, podcast guest slots |
| 100k | Month 24 | All of the above plus one signature series people wait for |

If the current count is already above 5k, pull each milestone forward a quarter.

## What gets measured (Metrics tab, weekly)

Followers, profile views, impressions per post, comments per post, DMs that mention a post, clinic bookings and workshop seats sold that week. Followers is the vanity number. Comments and bookings are the real ones.

## Contacts

Weekly list of ten in `contacts/`. Three groups:
1. **Amplifiers**: bigger SA and global founder voices to comment on daily, not to pitch.
2. **Operators**: people in golf tech, beverage, FMCG, coaching and SA business media who should know Johannes exists.
3. **This week's people**: anyone he actually dealt with this week. Those names stay in the private sheet.

LinkedIn's API does not let a tool send connection requests or comments on his behalf. Each name comes with a two-line note he can paste. Ten minutes on a Sunday evening.

## Not negotiable

- Nothing about a deal, partner, ambassador, investor or insurer term that Johannes has not announced himself.
- No customer data. Course names are fine; golfer names and numbers are not.
- Nothing that reads as legal, insurance or financial advice.
- This repo is public. Private material lives in the sheet.
