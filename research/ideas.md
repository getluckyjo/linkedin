# Ideas and models bank

Running list. Each entry: the idea, the source, the Johannes angle. The Wednesday post draws from here.

## Added 13 Oct 2026

### Hole-in-one challenge economics worldwide
- Floating tee in a lake, $1 a ball, $10k prize: estimated ~$600k a year from one pontoon (The Koerner Office breakdown, Facebook, 2026).
- New Zealand lakeside hole-in-one business, ~$650k a year (YouTube, "How to Make Millions with a Hole in One Challenge").
- Reddit r/EntrepreneurRideAlong: "$20 golf challenge printing hundreds", $10k prize.
- U.S. Hold the Green Challenge sells it as a dealer franchise: par-3 contests run by local operators (franchisegator.com).
- LIV Golf x HSBC: Bryson DeChambeau hitting into Victoria Harbour, Hong Kong, as a hole-in-one activation (Sports Business Journal, Feb 2026). LIV sponsorship revenue passed $500M in 2025.
- **Johannes angle:** same model everywhere; the edge is insurance plus cameras plus distribution through venues. Used in post 2026-10-14.

### Micro-SaaS after the 2026 "SaaS reset"
- About $2 trillion wiped off software market caps Jan to Feb 2026; 70% of micro-SaaS earn under $1k MRR, the median profitable one ~$4.2k MRR (startupa.ge citing Freemius).
- Survivors have a data moat, a community moat or a network effect. "Boring, specific frictions for a group you can actually reach and who already pays for tools" (getcreatr.com).
- Lovable guide: niche SaaS run by 1 to 5 people, $50k to $3M a year, launched in 4 to 12 weeks.
- **Johannes angle:** 17 things shipped since March without coding. Which would survive an AI update? The ones with a venue, a camera and an insurer behind them. Software is the cheap part now.

### Service-backed software (sell it, deliver by hand, then build)
- Sell a narrow offer first, take deposits or paid pilots, deliver manually, automate once customers pay (blog.mean.ceo, Aug and Oct 2026).
- Examples cited: Zoho (~$1.4B revenue, self-funded), Atlassian (started on a credit card), Mailchimp, Basecamp.
- **Johannes angle:** corporate golf days and school fundraisers were sold before the automation existed. The Duchess got onto shelves by cold-calling heads of beverage. Launch, learn, repeat is the SA version of this.

### Local experience platforms in less-travelled places
- US Chamber 2026 list: slow travel and interest-based experiences in rural areas; 65% of seasoned travellers say hands-on activities influence where they go.
- **Johannes angle:** Garden Route and Winelands courses are already experience venues. A R100,000 shot is an experience product. Tour operators earn 20% on Get Lucky entries today.

### LinkedIn as a fundraising channel for SA founders
- Launch Base Africa (Sep 2026): founders use LinkedIn to reach investors directly; one SA founder's launch post brought several inbound investors.
- **Johannes angle:** build in public is deal flow, not vanity. Say so once any round is public.

## Formats worth stealing
- "Analysed 397,605 posts in Q1": data-led posts about LinkedIn itself perform. Once a quarter, post the engine's own numbers.
- Carousels get ~3.7x the engagement of text (Cleverly, 2026). One a week from month 3.
- Reply to every comment in the first 90 minutes; a comment weighs roughly 15x a like in distribution.
