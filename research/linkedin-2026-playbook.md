# LinkedIn in 2026: what the data says, and what we do about it

Compiled 13 October 2026 from the four big benchmark studies published this year plus the algorithm write-ups. Every number below is from a vendor that sells LinkedIn software, so treat them as directional. Where studies disagree, the disagreement is noted.

## The big picture

- **Reach is down for everyone.** The 2026 van der Blom report (1.3M posts, H1 2026) reports views down ~50%, engagement down ~25%, follower growth down ~59% year on year. Posting more does not fix it. Posting on one topic, consistently, with posts people stay on, does.
- **A normal post on a personal profile:** median 412 impressions, 10 engagements, 2.3% engagement rate (ContentIn, 100k posts from 1,055 real profiles). Anything above 7.65% is a top-10% post. Use these as the baseline in the Metrics tab, not the 5% "averages" quoted elsewhere.
- **What the algorithm now rewards:** dwell time (people stopping and reading or swiping), comments longer than a few words, replies to comments, saves, and topic consistency. What it punishes: external links in the body (studies range from 19% to 60% less reach), reshares, LinkedIn articles, generic AI-sounding text.
- **Follower count does not rescue you.** Median engagement rate falls as accounts get bigger (2.56% under 1k followers, 1.53% over 100k). Growth comes from posts, not from having grown before.

## Formats, personal profiles only (AuthoredUp, 3M posts, Mar 2025 to Feb 2026)

| Format | Reach vs your own median | Engagement vs your own median | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Document (PDF carousel) | 1.39x | 1.30x | Best overall. Only 4.9% of profiles post them. 12.9% of all saves. |
| Image | 1.20x | 1.33x | Highest engagement multiplier. Real photos, screenshots, a chart. |
| Poll | 1.78x | 0.37x | Reach trap. Votes are not conversations. |
| Text only | 1.07x | 0.78x | Works for strong writers with a built audience (top 5% get 1.26x reach). Everyone else, below average. |
| Video | 0.86x | 0.93x | Down 36% year on year. Only 3 minutes plus beats the median. 30 to 60 second clips are the worst bucket. |
| Article | 0.69x | 0.44x | Dead. |
| Reshare | 0.29x | 0.22x | Dead. |

ContentIn's within-author data says simpler: any media beats no media by roughly 3x on median engagement (2.7 to 2.9% vs 1.54%), and image, video and document are statistically indistinguishable from each other. Ligo (134k posts) found images get 36% more engagement than the same author's text posts.

Company-page studies (Socialinsider, 1.3M posts from 16k pages) rank documents first at 7.0%, multi-image 6.45%, video 6.0%, image 5.3%, text 4.5%. Same ordering, higher numbers, different population.

## Images specifically

- No 2026 study isolates AI-generated images against real photos. The practitioner consensus is consistent though: a real photo of the person, a screenshot of a real number, or a chart beats a generated illustration. Generic AI visuals read as AI, and AI-sounding content gets 9% to 21% fewer impressions at equal substance (MagicPost within-author study of 40,937 posts; Ligo).
- Teract claims single-image posts get 20 to 30% less reach than text; no other study corroborates it. Ignore it.
- Multi-image posts (3 to 4 real photos) are the quiet winner on company pages (6.9% in Q2 2026) and rising.

**Rule for Johannes:** never a generated illustration, never stock. Use what he already has that nobody else has: 4G camera stills and clips of real hole-in-ones, the tee box set-ups, the workshop room, a screenshot of a real dashboard. Branding belongs on the carousel template, not on a picture.

## Writing

- AI-sounding text costs reach. Ligo: 21% less engagement than the same author's human-sounding posts. The tells people flag: tidy numbered lists, triplets, "here's what I learned", a question bolted on the end, em dashes, no rough edges.
- Hook plus dwell: the first two lines decide the click on "see more"; the body has to be worth 20 seconds. Short is fine; thin is not.
- One topic. The algorithm in 2026 treats accounts as authorities on something. Johannes's something is "simple businesses that make money, launch learn repeat, from South Africa". Golf, drinks and coaching are the examples, not the topic.
- Reply to every comment within 60 to 90 minutes; a reply creates a nested thread and counts as a comment. Comments weigh far more than reactions (one widely cited study says 15x; take the direction, not the number).
- No links in the body. Put the link in the first comment, or make the post good enough that people search. For the workshop, write the URL as plain text without https so it does not render as a link card.

## Cadence and timing

- 3 posts a week is the sweet spot for growth without diluting reach. Five a week only pays if every post is strong.
- Early engagement velocity (first 60 to 90 minutes) still sets distribution. Post when Johannes can be on his phone for the next hour. Tuesday to Thursday mornings SAST remain the safest window for a South African audience with UK and US overlap later in the day.
- Never two posts on one day. Count his own manual posts.

## The format mix we run

| Slot | Format | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Post 1 | PDF carousel, 6 to 9 slides, from the week's strongest idea | Best reach and saves on personal profiles, almost nobody does it, dwell time is built in |
| Post 2 | 1 to 4 real photos plus short text | Highest engagement multiplier, stops the scroll, cheap to make from what he already has |
| Post 3 | Text only, 400 to 900 characters | His voice is the asset; keep one slot for pure writing |

Video only when there is a real 3-minute story (a hole-in-one reaction, a workshop walk-through). No 30-second talking heads.

## Series worth owning

1. **Hole-in-one of the week.** The camera clip or still, the course, the tier, what it cost to enter and what it paid. Needs the golfer's ok. Nobody else in SA has this footage.
2. **Model of the week.** One business model from anywhere in the world, taken apart in a carousel. The floating tee is the first one.
3. **The one I got wrong.** A failure from Brannas, DOPE, Duchess, Cape Spritz or Get Lucky and what it cost. Honest beats polished, and it is the talk he already gives.
4. **Shipped this week.** What got built, in a screenshot. Continues the "17 things since March" thread.

## Profile (what converts a visitor into a follower)

- Headline: keep "Building Get Lucky Golf | Coaching a handful of exceptional founders | ex-Duchess, ex-DOPE". Consider leading with the promise: "Simple businesses that make money. Launch, learn, repeat. | Get Lucky Golf | ex-Duchess, ex-DOPE".
- Banner: a real photo from a tee box with the R1 million sign, not a designed graphic.
- Featured: pin the best carousel, the workshop page, the Duchess deck page.
- Creator mode on, topics set to Entrepreneurship, Small Business, Startups. Follow button first, not Connect.
